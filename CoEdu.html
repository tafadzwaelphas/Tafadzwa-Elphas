Pitch Strategy

Goals

Online learners with limited technological proficiency struggle to navigate educational platforms, which lowers course engagement and blunts the impact institutions are trying to have. CoEdu's answer was to meet students where they already are — letting them access course content over WhatsApp and Telegram, with automated handling of common student inquiries, rather than asking tech-limited learners to adopt yet another platform. I designed the brand (logo, color system, typography) and the web and app UI end to end, and worked with the team on the funding pitch itself, presented as a $100,000 ask for a 24-month runway, split across marketing, product development, and operations. At the time of the pitch, CoEdu had introduced the product to 11 institutions across Ghana and neighboring markets, with 7 early adopters.
