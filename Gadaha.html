Goals

In Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, and Qatar, about 55% of women struggle with obesity — among the highest rates globally — and cardiovascular disease is the leading cause of death across the Gulf (source: National Library of Medicine). Gadaha set out to build a fitness app for that gap: one designed around hijab and modest dress rather than against it, pairing an AI-guided virtual trainer, meal tracking with in-app healthy food ordering, and workout communities. The process started with a moodboard — competitor screens, gym photography, and a color and iconography direction — before moving into the app itself, structured flow by flow: onboarding, workouts, meals, community, account. Alongside the UI, I worked with the team on positioning and go-to-market thinking — who the app was for, and how to reach them through influencer partnerships on Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat.
